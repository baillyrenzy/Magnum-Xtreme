FAQ

How long does one bottle last?

Thirty days at two gummies per day (60 gummies, 30 servings).

Should I talk to a doctor first?

Yes, especially if you take prescription medication or have a heart, prostate, hormone or blood pressure condition. Saw Palmetto and Horny Goat Weed can interact with some medicines.

Are there side effects?

Herbal supplements can cause stomach upset, headaches or allergic reactions in some people. Stop use and contact a healthcare professional if you feel unwell.

Is this a medicine for erectile dysfunction?

No. It is a dietary supplement and not a treatment for any condition. See a doctor for ongoing sexual health concerns.

How fast will it work?

There is no guaranteed timeline. People respond differently, and the label shows no clinical results.

Does it contain sugar?

Yes: 3 g per serving, including added sugars. Check the label if you monitor sugar intake.

Can women or anyone under 18 use it?

No. It is formulated for adult men.
